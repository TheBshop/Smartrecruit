NCSA vs Smartrecruit: a comparison for international athletes

Updated: October 31, 2026

NCSA is a U.S. recruiting service with paid memberships; Smartrecruit is a free website for doing the process on your own, built for athletes from Latin America. Each serves a different profile. Here are the differences, without exaggeration.

The short answer

If you want a service with guidance and are willing to pay for a membership, NCSA is a well-known option. If you prefer to do the process yourself, for free, with a focus on athletes from Latin America, Smartrecruit is built for that. Both are independent of the NCAA and of the universities.

When each makes sense

Smartrecruit if you want to save money, understand each step and stay in control, and if you are comfortable with a Spanish-language app. A paid service like NCSA if you want to delegate part of the work and value the guidance. In any case, no service can guarantee a scholarship.

Before paying for any service

Ask in writing what the price includes, how long the contract lasts, what happens if you receive no offers and how to cancel. More questions in the agency comparison.

Frequently asked questions

Is NCSA free?

Its site offers paid memberships. Prices and conditions change, so confirm them on its official page.

Is Smartrecruit affiliated with NCSA?

No. Smartrecruit is independent and is not affiliated with NCSA, the NCAA, the NAIA or any university.

Do I need to pay to contact a coach?

No. Coaches' emails are public and you can write to them directly on your own.

Can I use Smartrecruit and another service at the same time?

Yes. Smartrecruit is free and has no exclusivity.
