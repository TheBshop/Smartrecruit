Get a U.S. college sports scholarship on your own, for free

Smartrecruit is a website for pursuing a college sports scholarship in the United States without an agency. It shows which universities recruit your position, the official emails of their coaches and the searches coaches have posted, and it helps you write to them in English.

Language note: the Smartrecruit app (profile, university matching, coach contacts and email generator) is currently in Spanish. The emails it generates for coaches are always in English. These guides and the university directory are in English.

What it is, and what it is not

It is a free tool and a set of guides for athletes in NCAA, NAIA and NJCAA recruiting, covering soccer, basketball, volleyball, softball, field hockey, rugby and more. It is not an agency or an intermediary, it does not guarantee scholarships or admission, it does not charge for its use, and it is not affiliated with the NCAA, the NAIA, the NJCAA or any university.
