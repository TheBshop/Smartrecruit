How many international players are on U.S. college rosters?

Published October 31, 2026

On the rosters we analyzed, 36.6% of men's soccer players are international; in basketball, between 14% and 16%. These are Smartrecruit figures from official rosters, without names. Here is the data, how we built it and its limits.

The numbers by sport

We count players with a country of origin listed on each program's official roster, and treat anyone not from the U.S. as international. Only programs with at least 8 players and a country listed for 80% or more of the roster are included.

What stands out

Men's soccer is the most international sport. More than a third of players come from abroad, led by England, Spain, Germany and Canada, and there are 326 players from Latin America on the rosters analyzed. Field hockey is a special case: Argentina is the second country of origin, with 51 players, behind the Netherlands. In basketball Canada, Australia and, in women's basketball, Spain lead. Latin America has little weight in basketball and women's soccer.

How to read this data

These are the programs for which we have full rosters with country of origin, not every NCAA program, and the mix of divisions changes by sport. Men's basketball combines Division I and II programs. Rosters change every season and the country comes from what each university publishes (sometimes it is the country of the hometown, not of nationality). Use it as a trend reference, not as a quota or a guarantee of a spot.

What to do with this

If a program already has players from your country or region, it is often a sign that it recruits in your area: mention it in your email. Frequently asked questions

Are there many international players in U.S. college soccer?

On the rosters we analyzed, 36.6% of men's soccer players and 11.1% of women's soccer players are international.

Where do most international players come from?

It depends on the sport: in men's soccer, England, Spain, Germany and Canada; in basketball, Canada and Australia; in field hockey, the Netherlands and Argentina.

Does this data include every university?

No. Only programs with a full roster and a country of origin listed for at least 80% of players. It is a reference, not the whole NCAA.

Where can I see this for a specific university?

On each university's page in Smartrecruit, in the international players section.
