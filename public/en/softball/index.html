How to use this list

Each row is a coach's post looking for players at a position and graduation year. Class is the year the player finishes high school. If you finish in December 2026 and start college in August 2027, you generally fit the class of 2027: confirm with each coach. Posts are days or weeks old, so the spot may already be filled: check the date and email the coach anyway if your profile fits.

Which divisions appear

Most needs come from junior colleges (NJCAA and regional associations such as CCCAA and NWAC) and the NAIA; NCAA Division I has very few. To understand what each division offers, read how U.S. college softball works and the guides.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these needs come from?

They are posts by each program's coaches. Smartrecruit gathers and sorts them and adds the coach's email; it is not part of any college.

Can an international player apply?

Yes: colleges can recruit international athletes, subject to their association's rules and each school's admission. Check eligibility before writing.

How often is it updated?

About every three days. The data date is shown above.
